How I approach an ad account

Most underperforming accounts share one root cause: weak data. If Event Match Quality is low or purchases are double counted, the algorithm is optimising blind and no creative will save it. So the first week is about the foundation.

After that it is a testing system. New angles go into a dedicated test, winners graduate to the scaling campaign, and retargeting is split by intent. On one Canadian health brand, splitting retargeting into cart abandoners, product viewers and site visitors pushed the cart abandoner tier to 5.52x ROAS. On an apparel store, a seven day catalog test cut CPM from $87 to $32.50 and lifted CTR to 8.55%.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Meta Ads management cost?+
Setups and audits start around 50 USD on Fiverr. Monthly management is quoted on ad spend, number of markets and creative volume, as a fixed price.
What ad spend do I need?+
You can start testing from around 20 to 30 USD a day. Below that, Meta struggles to exit the learning phase on purchase optimisation.
How long until I see results?+
The first 7 to 14 days are a learning and testing phase. Clearer results usually show in weeks 2 to 4 if tracking is correct and the offer converts.
Do you make the ad creatives?+
I write the angles, hooks and briefs and can produce static and edited video ads. For UGC I brief your creators or recommend ones that fit your budget.
