What I build

  • Meta Lead Ads to WhatsApp and CRM, with routing by service, location or answer, in seconds
  • Automated ad reports delivered daily or weekly to Slack, email or Google Sheets
  • Tracking and budget alerts that flag broken pixels and overspend before they cost money
  • Shopify and Klaviyo workflows for orders, reviews and customer follow up
  • AI steps that classify leads, draft replies and summarise data, always with a human approving
  • AI agents and RAG systems with LangChain and LangGraph for support and internal knowledge

Why n8n

n8n is open source, can be self hosted and has no per task pricing, so it stays affordable as volume grows. It connects to almost any API and handles complex logic that gets expensive or fragile in other tools. I also work with Make and Zapier, and migrate workflows between them.

Example: a self healing ad monitor

An n8n workflow polls each live ad account on a schedule, checks Event Match Quality, event volume and budget pacing, and when something drifts an LLM step writes a plain language diagnosis. Nothing touches spend until a person approves. Problems surface in hours instead of days.
