FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does Meta Ads management cost?+
Setups, audits and tracking fixes start around $50 on Fiverr. Monthly management is quoted on your ad spend, number of markets and how much creative you need. Send your store link and you get a fixed quote, not an hourly meter.
How fast will I see results?+
Meta needs data before it optimises well. Expect the first 7 to 14 days to be a learning and testing phase, with clearer results in weeks 2 to 4, as long as tracking is correct and the offer converts. I will tell you honestly if the offer or the store is the bottleneck.
Can you set up Conversions API on Shopify?+
Yes. Pixel plus Conversions API through the Shopify Meta channel or server side tagging, with deduplication, domain verification and Event Match Quality pushed as high as your data allows. I also work with WooCommerce, Salla, ThriveCart and custom stacks.
What access do you need?+
Partner access to your ad account through Meta Business Manager and collaborator access on Shopify. You never share passwords, you can remove access at any time, and nothing that changes spend goes live without your approval.
Do you only run ads?+
No. I’m also a machine learning engineer, so I build n8n automations, WhatsApp and CRM lead routing, reporting pipelines and AI agents that work alongside your ads.
Which time zones do you work with?+
Clients in the US, Canada, UK, Europe, Australia and the Gulf. Communication is message first with clear written updates, so time zones are rarely an issue.
